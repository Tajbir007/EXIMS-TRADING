ABOUT EXIMS TRADING

Built on relationships.

A TRADING BUSINESS WITH A PRACTICAL PURPOSE

Business is built
in the details.

At Exims Trading, export, import and supply form the foundation of our work. Medical and surgical products are a significant area of focus, supported by our Safety and Surgifit brands and selected international products.

Our network involves customer relationships across Bangladesh's divisions, districts and local markets, with international connections including India, China and Malaysia. These are trading relationships, not claims of branch offices.

VALUES / RESPONSIBILITY / TRUST

Quality products and lasting trust are the two things I want Exims Trading to be known for. Since 2008, I have worked with the belief that a company's reputation is built not just on what it delivers, but on how consistently it stands behind its promises. For me, business has always been about creating value through genuine products, honest practices, and dependable relationships.

The road hasn't always been smooth. Years of challenges, setbacks, and difficult decisions have shaped both me and Exims Trading. But my principles have never changed. I would rather accept a loss than compromise on quality or sell a product I cannot stand behind. I believe trust is earned through actions, and no short-term profit is worth putting that trust at risk.

My vision is to make Exims Trading a renowned and trusted name in Bangladesh, recognized for the quality of its products, the strength of its relationships, and the integrity of its work. I dream of a day when our name becomes a source of pride for Bangladesh and an example for businesses beyond our borders. We have come a long way, but I believe our greatest achievements are still ahead.
